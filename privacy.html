Privacy & Affiliate Disclosure

Last updatedSeptember 2026

This policy explains what data RAW/FORGE collects, how referral links work, and what you can expect from us. Short version: we collect almost nothing, and some links earn us a commission.

Affiliate & referral disclosure

Some links on this site are referral (affiliate) links. If you click one and make a purchase or download a file, the author of this site may receive a commission or other compensation from the seller — at no extra cost to you. The price you pay is exactly the same.

In practice this means:

  • Model source-file links usually point to Creative Fabrica and include a referral reference.
  • Hardware and printer banners may point to marketplaces such as AliExpress and may also be sponsored.
  • We only link files we consider worth printing. Compensation never decides what gets listed — the curation comes first, the referral comes second.

As a matter of honesty: buying through a referral link is one of the ways the site pays for itself. If you prefer not to support it that way, you can always find the same files by searching their titles directly — we would rather you print than not.

What we collect

RAW/FORGE runs as a static site. We do not use accounts, contact forms, comments, or analytics cookies, and we do not build profiles of visitors. Your browsing here leaves no data with us.

Two honest exceptions you should know about:

  • Web server logs. Like any website, our hosting may automatically record standard technical information (IP address, browser type, requested pages) for security and error handling. This is routine infrastructure logging, not profiling, and it is kept for a short period.
  • Third-party destinations. When you click an external link — Creative Fabrica, AliExpress, or any other marketplace — their own privacy policies take over. Those sites may use cookies and affiliate tracking to attribute the referral. We have no access to or control over that data; review their policies before purchasing.

Cookies

RAW/FORGE itself sets no cookies. If you arrive from a referral platform, that platform may set its own cookies on the destination site after you click through. Nothing is stored by us.

Children

3D printing is a family-friendly hobby, and much of the catalog suits younger makers with adult supervision. This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone — child or adult.

Content & trademarks

3D models listed here are designed by independent creators and hosted on their marketplace platforms. RAW/FORGE curates and links; we do not claim ownership of the designs. Product names and brands belong to their respective owners and are used for identification only.

Changes to this policy

If the site adds analytics, forms, or new partner programs, this page will be updated to say so plainly, with the date at the top changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy or a referral link: reach the studio through the site footer. We answer plainly.
